# Blind Drop — light app-icon concepts

These are production-clean vector interpretations of four directions explored with the built-in image-generation workflow. They deliberately use the sealed-song palette only:

- background: `amberWash` / `#F6EAD6`
- mark: `amberDeep` / `#96590A`

All source canvases are square and full bleed. They do not include a rounded app-icon mask; iOS applies that mask at display time.

## Directions

1. **Drop slot** — the most direct expression of “drop a song”; playful and legible at small sizes.
2. **Sealed groove** — a record interrupted by a privacy band and an abstract seal; the clearest “hidden until later” concept.
3. **Record stamp** — closest to the app’s existing sealed-card visual language; calm, ownable, and brand-system friendly.
4. **Hidden sleeve** — a record peeking from an album sleeve; the most music-first and least game-like.

## Final prompt set

All four directions used the same production constraints: “iOS app icon for Blind Drop; full-bleed pale sealed-song amber wash; one deep-amber geometric mark; exceptionally crisp flat 2D vector style; strong original silhouette; readable at 29px; exactly two colors; no rounded outer container, text, letters, gradients, lighting, shadows, bevels, depth, texture, mockup, or watermark.” The subject prompt varied between a record dropping into a slot, a groove interrupted by a seal band, a double-ring record stamp, and a record peeking from a sleeve.
